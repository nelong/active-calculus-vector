Definition 13.6.5.

The divergence of a vector field
\begin{equation*} \vF(x,y)=\langle F_1(x,y,z),F_2(x,y,z), F_3(x,y,z)\rangle \end{equation*}
is given by
\begin{equation*} \divg(\vF)=\frac{\partial F_1}{\partial x}+\frac{\partial F_2}{\partial y} + \frac{\partial F_3}{\partial z}\text{.} \end{equation*}
In two dimensions, the definition is analogous by omiting the third term.
