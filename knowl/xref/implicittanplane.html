Key Idea 11.8.6.

Let \(S_1\) be an implicit surface given by \(F(x,y,z)=k\) and let \(P_0=(x_0,y_0,z_0)\) be a point on \(S_1\text{.}\) The tangent plane to \(S_1\) at \(P_0\) will be given by
\begin{equation*} \frac{\partial F}{\partial x}(P_0) (x-x_0)+ \frac{\partial F}{\partial y}(P_0) (y-y_0) +\frac{\partial F}{\partial z}(P_0) (z-z_0)=0 \end{equation*}

Proof.

By Assemblage  we need to find a normal vector for the plane and a point on the plane. The point \(P_0=(x_0,y_0,z_0)\) is the location where our tangent plane will intersect \(S_1\) so we only need to find a normal vector for our plane. By our interpretation of the gradient above, \(\nabla F (P_0)= \langle \frac{\partial F}{\partial x}(P_0), \frac{\partial F}{\partial y}(P_0), \frac{\partial F}{\partial z}\rangle\) will be perpendicular to the level surface \(S_1\text{.}\) Therefore,
\begin{equation*} \frac{\partial F}{\partial x}(P_0) (x-x_0)+ \frac{\partial F}{\partial y}(P_0) (y-y_0) +\frac{\partial F}{\partial z}(P_0) (z-z_0)=0 \end{equation*}
