Definition 11.3.12.

A function \(f=f(x,y)\) is continuous at the point \((a, b)\) provided that
  1. \(f\) is defined at the point \((a, b)\text{,}\)
    🔗
    🔗
  2. \(\displaystyle{\lim_{(x,y) \to (a,b)} f(x,y)}\) exists, and
    🔗
    🔗
  3. \(\displaystyle{\lim_{(x,y) \to (a,b)} f(x,y) = f(a,b)}\text{.}\)

    🔗
