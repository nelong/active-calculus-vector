Definition 11.4.4.

The first-order partial derivatives of \(f\) with respect to \(x\) and \(y\) at a point \((a,b)\) are, respectively,
\begin{align*} f_x(a,b) \amp = \frac{\partial f}{\partial x}(a,b) = \lim_{h \to 0} \frac{f(a+h,b)-f(a,b)}{h}\\ f_y(a,b) \amp = \frac{\partial f}{\partial y}(a,b)= \lim_{h \to 0} \frac{f(a,b+h)-f(a,b)}{h}\text{,} \end{align*}
provided the limits exist.
