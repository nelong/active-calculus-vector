Vector addition is commutative. This means we obtain the same sum if we place the tail of \(\vu\) at the tip of \(\vv\) as if we place the tail of \(\vv\) at the tip of \(\vu\text{.}\) We therefore see that \(\vu+\vv\) appears as the diagonal of the parallelogram determined by \(\vu\) and \(\vv\text{,}\) as shown in Figure 9.3.6(c).
