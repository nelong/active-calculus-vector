Exercise 13.

In this exercise we verify the curvature formula
\begin{equation*} \kappa = \frac{\vecmag{\vr'(t) \times \vr''(t)}}{\vecmag{\vr'(t) }^3}. \end{equation*}
🔗

(a)

Explain why
\begin{equation*} \vecmag{\vr'(t)} = \frac{ds}{dt}. \end{equation*}
🔗
🔗

(b)

Use the fact that \(\vT(t) = \frac{\vr'(t)}{\vecmag{\vr'(t)}}\) and \(\vecmag{\vr'(t)} = \frac{ds}{dt}\) to explain why
\begin{equation*} \vr'(t) = \frac{ds}{dt} \vT(t). \end{equation*}
🔗
🔗

(c)

The Product Rule shows that
\begin{equation*} \vr''(t) = \frac{d^2s}{dt^2} \vT(t) + \frac{ds}{dt} \vT'(t). \end{equation*}
Explain why
\begin{equation*} \vr'(t) \times \vr''(t) = \left(\frac{ds}{dt}\right)^2 (\vT(t) \times \vT'(t)). \end{equation*}
🔗
🔗

(d)

In Key Idea on \(\vT\) and \(\vN\) we showed that \(\vecmag{\vT(t)} = 1\) implies that \(\vT(t)\) is orthogonal to \(\vT'(t)\) for every value of \(t\text{.}\) Explain what this tells us about \(\vecmag{\vT(t) \times \vT'(t)}\) and conclude that
\begin{equation*} \vecmag{\vr'(t) \times \vr''(t)} = \left(\frac{ds}{dt}\right)^2 \vecmag{\vT'(t)}. \end{equation*}
🔗
🔗

(e)

Finally, use the fact that \(\kappa = \frac{\vecmag{\vT'(t)} }{\vecmag{\vr'(t)}}\) to verify that
\begin{equation*} \kappa = \frac{\vecmag{vr'(t) \times \vr''(t) }}{\vecmag{\vr'(t)}^3}. \end{equation*}
🔗
