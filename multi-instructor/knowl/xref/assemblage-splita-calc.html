Efficient Calculation of \(a_{\vT}, a_{\vN}, \vT, \vN\) in terms of \(\vv\) and \(\va\).

\begin{align*} \vT \amp= \frac{\vv}{\vecmag{\vv}} \\ a_{\vT} \amp= \frac{\va\cdot\vv}{\vecmag{\vv}} \Rightarrow a_{\vT} \vT = \frac{\va\cdot \vv}{\vv \cdot \vv} \vv\\ a_{\vN} \amp= \vecmag{ \va - a_{\vT} \vT } = \sqrt{\vecmag{ \va }^2 - (a_{\vT})^2}= \sqrt{(\va \cdot \va)- \frac{(\va\cdot\vv)^2}{\vecmag{\vv}}} \\ a_{\vN} \vN \amp= \va - a_{\vT} \vT \\ \vN \amp= \frac{\va - a_{\vT} \vT}{a_{\vN}} \end{align*}
