Definition 10.4.4.

The arc length of \(C\text{,}\) a curve in space, is given by
\begin{equation*} L=\lim_{n \rightarrow \infty} \sum_{i=1}^{n} \sqrt{(\Delta x_i)^2 + (\Delta y_i)^2 + (\Delta z_i)^2} \end{equation*}
where \(\Delta\vr_i= \vr_i-\vr_{i-1} = \langle \Delta x_i ,\Delta y_i , \Delta z_i \rangle \) such that \(\vr_i=\vr(t_i)\) where \(t_i=a+ i \Delta t\) with \(\Delta t= \frac{b-a}{n}\text{.}\)
