\(z = 9 - 3 x - 3 y\text{.}\) Note that the projection of this into the \(xy\)-plane is the triangular region below \(y = 3 - x\text{.}\) The mass of the volume is then
\begin{equation*} M = \int_R \delta\,dV = \int_0^3\int_0^{3 - x}\int_0^{9-3 x-3 y}\,x + 3 y\,dz\,dy\,dx. \end{equation*}
Integrating with respect to \(z\text{,}\)
\begin{equation*} M = \int_0^3\int_0^{3 - x} (x + 3 y)(9-3 x-3 y)\,dy\,dx \end{equation*}
so that
\begin{equation*} M = \int_0^3\int_0^{3 - x} -3 x^2 + 9 x - 3 x y - 9 x y + 27 y - 9 y^2\,dy\,dx. \end{equation*}
Then, integrating with respect to \(y\text{,}\)
\begin{equation*} M = \int_0^3 (-3 x^2 + 9 x)(3 - x) + \frac12(27 - 12 x)(3 - x)^2 - 3 (3 - x)^3\,dx. \end{equation*}
Expanding the these terms, we have
\begin{equation*} M = \int_0^3 0 x^3 + \frac{9}{2} x^2 - 27 x + \frac{81}{2}\, dx = \frac{81}{2}. \end{equation*}
