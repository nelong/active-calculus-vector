Key Idea 10.1.1. Classic Calculus Approach.

We highlight and reference the following steps as a fundamental approach to introducing and defining ideas in the calculus of both single variable and multivariable functions. We may abbreviate the Classic Calculus Approach as CCA .
🔗
CCA Step 1: Approximate the measurement of interest
🔗
CCA Step 2: Quantify how the approximation you made changes under a finer scale
🔗
CCA Step 3: Use a limit to show the approximation converges to the measurement of interest
