Activity 12.8.2.
Consider the region \(D\) in the first quadrant of the \(xy\)-plane bounded by the lines \(y=2x\) and \(y=x/2\) and the hyperbolas \(y=2/x\) and \(y=1/(2x)\text{.}\) A plot of the region is shown in Figure 12.8.3. Notice how this region is neither vertically simple nor horizontally simple, so computing a double integral over \(D\) using the variables \(x\) and \(y\) would require at least two iterated double integrals. However, this activity will guide you through considering how the change of coordinates given by
\begin{equation*} x=\sqrt{st} \quad \text{ and } \quad y =\sqrt{\frac{s}{t}} \end{equation*}
transforms \(D\text{.}\)
🔗
(a)
Find the coordinates of the four corner points of the region \(D\text{.}\)
🔗
🔗
(b)
Given the form of the change of variable equations above, it is not obvious how to change \((x,y)\) points into \((s,t)\) points. Verify that the following equations are satisfied by the change of variable equations:
\begin{equation*} yx=s \quad \text{and} \quad \frac{y}{x}=\frac{1}{t} \end{equation*}
Use these new relationships between the coordinates to convert each of these four corner points from \(xy\)-coordinates to \(st\)-coordinates. Then plot and label these points on the \(st\)-plane axes below.
🔗

The first quadrant in the \(st\)-plane. The axes are both marked with ranges from \(0\) to \(2.5\text{.}\)

(c)
The goal of a change of variables is for the curved boundaries of the region \(D\) to be transformed to horizontal and vertical lines in the \(st\)-plane. The locations of the points you plotted in the previous part should giv eyou hope that this will work. To confirm, we must examine how each of the boundary curves is transformed by the change of variables formula.
🔗
Use the change of variables equations to write \(y=2x\) in terms of \(s\) and \(t\text{.}\) Then solve for whichever of \(s\) or \(t\) is appropriate and plot the resulting line on the axes in part b.
🔗
🔗
(d)
Write each of the other boundary curves of \(D\) (\(y=\frac{2}{x}, y=\frac{1}{2x}, y=\frac{x}{2}\)) in terms of \(s\) and \(t\) coordinates, solve for either \(s\) or \(t\) as appropriate, and plot the resulting lines on the axes in part b.
🔗
🔗
(e)
The previous part verifies that the irregular region \(D\) in the \(xy\)-plane is transformed to a rectangle in the \(st\)-plane by the change of variables formula given. Some areas of the region \(D\) are stretched more than others in order to straighten the sides into the rectangle you have drawn. Write a couple of sentences to describe what part of \(D\) you think is stretched the most in the transformation from \(xy\)-coordinates to \(st\)-coordinates.
