Exercise 19.

We can adapt the arc length formula to curves in 2-space that define \(y\) as a function of \(x\) as the following activity shows.
🔗
Let \(y = f(x)\) define a smooth curve in 2-space. Parameterize this curve and use Equation (10.4.1) to show that the length of the curve defined by \(f\) on an interval \([a,b]\) is
\begin{equation*} \int_a^b \sqrt{1+[f'(t)]^2} \, dt. \end{equation*}
