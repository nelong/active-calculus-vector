Key Idea 12.6.4. Interpretations of Triple Integrals.

For a solid region \(S\) in \(\R^3\text{,}\) the following are common and important interpretations of triple integrals over \(S\text{:}\)
  1. The triple integral
    \begin{equation*} \displaystyle V(S) = \iiint_S 1 \, dV \end{equation*}
    represents the volume of the solid \(S\).
    🔗
    🔗
  2. If \(S\) has density given by \(\delta = \delta(x,y,z)\text{,}\) then \(\displaystyle \iiint_S \delta(x,y,z) \, dV\) is the mass of \(S\text{.}\)
    🔗
    🔗
  3. If \(S\) has density given by \(\delta = \delta(x,y,z)\text{,}\) then the center of mass \((\overline{x}, \overline{y}, \overline{z})\) can be computed as
    \begin{align*} \overline{x} \amp = \frac{\iiint_S x \ \delta(x,y,z) \, dV}{M},\\ \overline{y} \amp = \frac{\iiint_S y \ \delta(x,y,z) \, dV}{M}, \\ \overline{z} \amp = \frac{\iiint_S z \ \delta(x,y,z) \, dV}{M}, \end{align*}
    where \(M\) is the mass of \(S\text{.}\)
    🔗
    🔗
  4. The average value of a function \(f\) over \(S\) is given by
    \begin{equation*} f_{\operatorname{AVG}(S)} = \displaystyle \left(\frac{1}{V(S)} \right) \iiint_S f(x,y,z) \, dV\text{,} \end{equation*}
    where \(V(S)\) is the volume of the solid \(S\text{.}\)
    🔗
    🔗
